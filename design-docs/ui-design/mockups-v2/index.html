BizApps Contracts — v2 mockups

Three surfaces only, and deliberately so: these are the pieces MJ does not generate for us. Everything else on the Contract and Template forms is generated-form chrome plus metadata, so there is nothing there to design. Tokens and component structure are copied from MJ 6 (_tokens.scss) and from orders' form-panel stack, so what is drawn here is buildable.

  1. Contract form — hero, renewal terms, modifications, documents, lineage (items 5, 8)
  2. Modifications editor — zoomed, mid-edit, with the provision picker (item 8, the D-15 centrepiece)
  3. Renewal & expiry watchlist — columns and the notice countdown (item 12)

v1 mockups (billing worklist, contract create, workspace) are in ../mockups/ and describe the retired billing-engine design. They are kept for reference only.
