This is a contract sheet, not an order form. You are stating what the customer is entitled to and on what terms — product, how it is covered, which subscription type it becomes, over what window, at what negotiated price. No order is created here. When a term activates and its billing schedule comes due, the engine assembles the draft, prices it through orders, and materializes the order there. Nothing on this screen computes money.

Deliberately NOT the tabbed draft workspace, and not a wizard. Accounting's mj-workspace-card draft-tab strip earns its keep because journal entries are entered constantly and in parallel. Manual contract entry is explicitly the rare path — a handful a year — so multi-draft tabs would be chrome nobody uses, and a wizard would force a sequence the paper does not have. One card, one form, sections in the order a contract is actually read. The full workspace pattern stays where the volume is.

What this form is not. It is not an order form and it is not orders' form embedded. Every field here is a contract provision — including the subscription type, which is a negotiated choice (ContractLine.SubscriptionTypeID) rather than something the engine guesses when it materializes the subscription. What contracts deliberately does NOT do is compute: no totals, no tax, no proration, no resolved prices. Those belong to orders and stay there.
